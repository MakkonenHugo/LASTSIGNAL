### LASTSIGNAL is an game i have been making in unity from scratch
- I have 3D modeled every single thing you see, myself
- I have made more than half of the soundtracks in the game myself (some from pixabay)
- I have made the scripts myself (exept some are made with the help of ai and some just fully ai)

## The games storyline and objective
### Level 1
After watching the intro and pressing play in the main menu, you will find yourself in an gray office room.
Your first objective is to find your way out of there, you listen to the radio and the door is unlocked, you fall down and find yourself in the next level.
### Level 2
Level 2 is an dreamcore grass field, in the field there is many different objects to interact with.
You again, find the radio and go through the door into the house and get to the next level.

### Level 3
This level is different, because its an hallway with nothing inside but an table, after passing the table, the lights go out for an few seconds.
After the lights go back on, you will find out that the hallway has stretched, you walk to the door but find it locked.
Because there is clearly nothing there to do, you turn around, you walk and find an sign that wasn't there before, it says "Why do you want to leave?"
Now you find an radio that wasn't there before, you listen to it and turn around, only to find out that the sign has dissapeared.
Now you walk through the hallway again and find more stuff that wasnt there before.

### Level 4
Level 4 is an studio room made for filming movies, the radio tells you to look through the documents scattered around and find the highest number.
You submit the number to an doors code panel and get out of there.

### Level 5
In level 5 you will find an laptop, with the laptop you hack an program, and with the program you open the door out.
Then you are in another room with an surveilance monitor, with it you can see video feed of the places you just were at.

### Level 6
Level 6 is an warehouse that is kind of scary, the lights flicker and sometimes you can see an figure standing in the hallway,
The lighting is dark and inside there is an computer with an game where you have to remember passwords.

### Level 7
In level 7 you will find yourself in an giant hallway, you will listen to the radio and the radio says "ITS A TRAP!" "RUN!"
And chase music will start playing, red text will start flashing on the screen saying "DONT LOOK BACK" and "ITS COMING" and stuff like that
The camera is locked to simulate fear in the player.

### Level 8
Level 8 is an empty room with an desk and the radio, when listening to the radio, the lights dim and the camera locks to the front.
The radio will tell you that the guy leaving the messages is actually Jeff(the dark figure) and that you will need to destroy this simulation.
Then you are faced with two choices, 1 leave the simulation and free Jeff and everyone else,
or 2, stay in this world.

## Bad ending
if you choose two the screen will fade and you will be faced with an picture of you and Jeff, you were turned into Jeff too, for eternity.
The end credits start playing and an red text comes on the screen, "Why did you stay?"

## Good ending
If you choose leave, you will get teleported to an office room in the real world, the screen is not pixelated anymore.
In Level 9 (the office) you will need to check computers for vulnerabilities, and hack the vulnerable computer for the code for the door.

### Parkour
After level 9, alarms will start blazing and you will need to parkour your way to the main server room.
In the parkour sections you can sprint and jump to avoid obstacles.

## Good ending
In the main server room you will start an computer and navigate an actual operating system to delete the simulation.
After deleting the simulation, you will exit with the door and burn the place down.

## End credits
After burning the place down you will fade into the end credits, with the server room burning, and you can spot Jeff sometimes in the room thanking you for freeing him.


# Controls
In the main parts of the game you will use just WASD to move and your mouse to look around
You can interact with objects by looking at them and pressing E
